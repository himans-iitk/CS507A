## Dataset

- 1,564 coded agent-file units in 201 repositories (pilot, reliability and main sets; frozen in `coding/labels_v1.csv`).
- 1,226 labels started from an AI draft that the annotator reviewed (64 coverage corrections); 338 were coded blind, without any draft.
- Newcomer need (D1/D2) of gap units: first annotator 930; second annotator (both coded a gap) 266; model fill after a correction or gap 20.
- Agreement on the blind reliability set (first annotator vs. an independent LLM annotator (OpenAI Codex); 338 units, 40 repos): Cohen's κ = **0.78** [0.71, 0.85], weighted κ = 0.80, same label on 86.1% of units; per repository median 90.0%, 15 repos in full agreement.
- Note: the second annotator is an LLM, so this is human–LLM agreement, not agreement between two human coders.
